Delete Your Data

How to delete your GrowDay account and personal data.

Delete your account in the app

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly in GrowDay:

  1. Open GrowDay and go to My Account (tap your profile icon)
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  3. Confirm when prompted

Deletion happens immediately and cannot be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your GrowDay account (email or Sign in with Apple login)
  • Your profile and your baby's profile (name, date of birth, birth week, gender)
  • Your activity history and milestone progress

If you signed in with Apple, GrowDay's access to your Apple ID is also revoked.

Your subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. To cancel it, open Settings on your iPhone → tap your name → Subscriptions → GrowDay → Cancel Subscription.

Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app or your account, email us from the address linked to your GrowDay account and ask us to delete your data. We'll delete it and confirm by email within 30 days.
