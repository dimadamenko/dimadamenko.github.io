Frequently Asked Questions

GrowDay subscriptions are managed by Apple through the App Store. To cancel:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top, then tap Subscriptions
  3. Find GrowDay and tap it
  4. Tap Cancel Subscription

You can also manage subscriptions directly in the App Store → your profile icon → Subscriptions. Your access will continue until the end of the current billing period.

If you've switched to a new device or reinstalled the app:

  1. Open GrowDay and sign in with the same account you used when you subscribed
  2. If the paywall appears, tap Restore purchases at the bottom of the screen

Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID or email account that was used for the original purchase. If you're still having trouble, contact us below.

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the app:

  1. Open GrowDay and go to My Account (tap your profile icon)
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete Account
  3. Confirm when prompted

This will permanently remove your account, baby profile, activity history, and milestone progress. This action cannot be undone. Note that deleting your account does not automatically cancel your subscription — please cancel it separately in Settings.

To update your baby's name, date of birth, birth week, or gender:

  1. Open GrowDay and go to My Account (tap your profile icon)
  2. Tap Edit My Info
  3. Update the details and tap Save

The app will immediately recalculate your baby's age-appropriate activities and milestones based on the updated information.
